Ordered Systems Lab

We design dependable computer systems for cloud, distributed, and ML platforms. Our work combines systems building with principled analysis to detect, localize, and prevent complex failures.

Research Pillars

We focus on reliability and fault tolerance across modern systems stacks. Projects are designed to be practical, measurable, and deployable.

Problem-Driven

We start from concrete failures seen in real systems and design robust solutions with direct operational value.

Cross-Layer Methods

Our projects bridge operating systems, program analysis, software engineering, and machine learning workflows.

Impact-Seeking

We work with industry partners and evaluate ideas in realistic environments to maximize external impact.

What We Build

From runtime checkers to fault injection frameworks, we create tools that make complex system behavior observable and debuggable.
